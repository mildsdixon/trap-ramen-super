# Super Trap Street-Luxe

The approved **Super Version** visual direction for Trap Ramen.

- Poster-like, mobile-first composition
- Near-black, lacquer red, warm gold, and clean white palette
- Oversized condensed display typography with brush-script accents
- Abundant, close-cropped food photography
- Thick rules, distressed ink texture, sharp keylines, and slight rotation
- Compact, commerce-first flow: order, menu, rotating drops, gallery, location

Desktop adaptation uses wider editorial bands while preserving the same energy, palette, type scale, and food-first hierarchy.
